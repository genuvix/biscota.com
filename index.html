Process

How a project runs

Week 1–2

Understand the problem

We talk to the people who will use the software, look at what exists today and write down what the first version needs to do. You get a short written scope and a fixed estimate.

Ongoing

Build in small pieces

Work is delivered every one to two weeks to a test environment you can open and click through. You see progress early and can change direction without losing much.

Launch

Release carefully

We move data, train your team where it's needed and release in stages. Nothing goes live on a Friday afternoon.

After

Keep it running

Most clients stay on a support plan. If you'd rather take the work in-house, we hand over the code, documentation and access, and walk your developers through it.

About

A small company, on purpose

Biscota is a small team of developers and designers. We keep it that way so that every project gets experienced people rather than whoever happens to be free.

We prefer plain, well-tested technology over whatever is new this year. The software we write should still be easy to read and change in five years, whether we are the ones maintaining it or not.

  • You own all code and designs we produce for you.
  • You talk to the people doing the work, not an account manager.
  • You get written estimates before work starts, and invoices that match them.
